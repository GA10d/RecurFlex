\section{Conclusion}
RecurFlex combines high-frequency Morlet power, four signed low-frequency bands, multiscale convolutions, and residual bottleneck recurrence. It achieves an official BCI four-finger correlation of 0.7741, a BCI five-finger mean of 0.7835, and a nine-subject Miller five-finger mean of 0.6432 after fresh two-thirds training. Controlled experiments support spectral fusion and low-frequency band separation. Matched-context baseline evaluation and causal adaptation remain future work.
